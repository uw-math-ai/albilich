# Strict verification of the endpoint-support lattice route

Verdict: correct with no gaps.

The proof supplies the required existential witness \(G=D_{\langle 2\rangle}([0,1])\) and the alternating-slope normalizer \(h\), and the integrated premise establishes that \(\alpha_h(f)=h^{-1}fh\) is an order automorphism. For an arbitrary order-preserving embedding into an arbitrary source-defined ambient Dlab group, the source taxonomy on Zenkov--Medvedev 1999, p. 546, together with Medvedev 2001, Russian p. 135 and English p. 75, reduces the ambient group by an order isomorphism to one of the six native interval or extended-real transformation models. This preserves injectivity, order, and the conjugation equation.

The perfectness step is valid: Dlab 1968, Theorem 4.3, p. 603, makes the nonabelian witness simple and hence perfect. The endpoint-germ quotients in Zenkov--Medvedev 1999, Propositions 1.1 and 1.2, are abelian, while the affine quotient in Proposition 2.2 is metabelian. Therefore the embedded witness lies in the first-characteristic kernel. Theorem 2.1 supplies the corresponding first-breakpoint orders in the one-sided extended-real case. Thus the least action component detects the inherited order in every listed ambient model.

The retained carrier argument is complete. Dlab 1968, Lemma 3.6, p. 599, gives uniqueness of a commuting locally right-linear map from its initial gradient on a simple support component; this makes each such component centralizer abelian. Consequently commuting perfect local subgroups have disjoint carriers. Fragmentation then gives finite-cover unions and the carrier intersection identity. Nested fragmentation defines a continuous equivariant surjection \(\pi\); the hill-and-valley argument proves monotonicity, separability makes every fiber a singleton, and the transferred Dlab order excludes decreasing orientation on the least component.

A hypothetical ambient implementer preserves that least component. Transport through the increasing coordinate \(\pi\) identifies its restriction with \(h\), because the centralizer of the standard locally moving action is trivial. The support components of \(h\) are \(C_m=(2^{-(2m+3)},2^{-(2m+1)})\) for \(m\geq 0\), with \(C_{m+1}<C_m\); hence they have no least member. Increasing conjugacy makes their inverse images full support components of the ambient implementer. This contradicts the well ordering of support components in every native Dlab model. The argument covers all source-listed endpoint variants, extended-real variants, inverse first-characteristic orders, unbounded action components, and abstract ordered copies.
