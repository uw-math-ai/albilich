# Verification report

The selected route is correct with no gaps. If an element of GL(V) fixes every Z-orbit of nonzero vectors, comparison on basis vectors and their pairwise sums forces it to be a single scalar from Z; hence the induced GL(V)/Z and SL(V)/Z actions are faithful. Since Z is contained in SL(V), the quotient index is [GL(V):SL(V)]=q-1, which is greater than one under q>2.

For arbitrary A in GL(V) and an arbitrary ordered triple represented by v1,v2,v3, their span W has dimension at most three. The hypothesis n>=4 gives a nonzero complement. On the decomposition V=A(W) direct sum A(U), an invertible map D can be chosen to fix A(W) pointwise and have determinant det(A)^{-1} on A(U). Then B=DA lies in SL(V) and satisfies Bvi=Avi for all three entries. Thus every A sends every ordered triple into its SL(V)-orbit, including repeated and dependent triples. Consequently GL(V)/Z preserves each G-orbit on Omega^3 and is contained in G^(3). The proper inclusion G<GL(V)/Z therefore yields G<G^(3) in a faithful action, proving that PSL_n(q) is not totally 3-closed.

The withdrawn stronger assertion X<G^(3) is not used in this implication. No external result or finite computation is needed.
